Privacy Policy

Telugu Panchangam (mytelugupanchangam.space) — last updated August 15, 2026.

This app shows the Telugu calendar, festivals, and astronomy information. It is built to work without accounts, sign-ins, or advertising.

What we collect

  • No personal information. There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts. (Names typed into the on-screen sankalpam helper stay on your device and are never sent to us.)
  • An anonymous device identifier. The app generates a random identifier, stored only on your device, used to remember your notification preferences and to count daily usage (for example, how many devices opened the app on a given day). It is not linked to you and cannot identify you.
  • Usage counts. We record which pages are viewed, the app language, and your time zone, tied only to the anonymous identifier above. We use no third-party analytics services, advertising identifiers, or cookies.
  • Error reports. If the app malfunctions, a technical error report (stack trace, browser type) may be sent to Sentry, our error-tracking processor, so we can fix bugs. These reports are not used for advertising.
  • Notifications. If you enable festival notifications, your device's push subscription is stored so we can deliver them. Disabling notifications deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking across other apps or sites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No location access — city and time zone settings you choose stay on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Clearing the app's site data (or uninstalling the app) removes the device identifier from your device, which permanently disconnects any stored preferences from it. To have server-side data removed, contact us with your request.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
